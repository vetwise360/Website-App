# Nonprofit, Faith, Education & Community

**Audience**: donors, volunteers, members, parents. They need *the mission, the impact, belonging, and one clear way to get involved*.
**Primary conversion**: Donate / Plan a visit / Enroll / Volunteer (pick one from the brief) · secondary: Learn more / events.

## Pick one direction
**A. Hopeful & Human** (default)
- paper `#fffcf7`, surface `#f5eee3`, ink `#1f2421`, muted `#58605b`, line `#e8dfd1`, brand `#1f4e4a`, brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#f2994a` (accent-ink `#1f1307`), deep `#173b38`.
- Fonts: **Young Serif** (`@fontsource/young-serif`) or **Fraunces** display + **Figtree** body. Real people photos, warm, impact numbers in big type.
**B. Calm Sanctuary** (churches, faith communities)
- paper `#f8f7f4`, brand `#2d3550`, accent `#c79a52` (accent-ink `#1c1407`), deep `#1c2133`. Fonts **EB Garamond** + **Work Sans**. Service times front and center.
**C. Bright Learning** (schools, daycares, tutoring, camps)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f3f7ff`, brand `#1e4fa3`, accent `#ffb020` (accent-ink `#1c1300`), deep `#122f63`. Fonts **Nunito** + **Nunito**/**Figtree**. Friendly rounded shapes, safety/credentials prominent.

## Homepage order
Header (Donate/Visit/Enroll button) → Hero (people photo, mission H1, CTAs, proof: years / people served) → Impact numbers (real) → Programs / ministries / classes → Story (one person's story) → Get involved (donate / volunteer / attend paths) → Events or service times → Testimonials → FAQ (first visit? where does money go?) → CTA band → Location/contact → Footer (EIN/registration if nonprofit).

## Avoid
Guilt-heavy imagery, walls of text, outdated event lists, tiny donate buttons, clip-art.
