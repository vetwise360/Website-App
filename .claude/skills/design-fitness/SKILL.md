# Fitness & Training

**Audience**: people ready to change (new year, new city, goal) who fear commitment and intimidation. They want *the vibe, the schedule/classes, coaches, price/trial, and location*.
**Primary conversion**: Claim a free class / Book a trial · secondary: View schedule / pricing.

## Pick one direction
**A. High-Energy Dark** (default — gyms, CrossFit, martial arts)
- paper `#0d0f12` (dark theme!), surface `#16191e`, ink `#f4f5f7`, muted `#a3a9b3`, line `#262a31`, brand `#f4f5f7`, brand-ink `#0d0f12`, accent `#d4ff3a` (electric lime, accent-ink `#0d0f12`) or `#ff4d2e`, deep `#000000`.
- Fonts: **Archivo** (800–900, uppercase H1) or **Oswald** + **Inter**. Big type, motion-safe reveals, diagonal crops, real action photos with dark overlay.
**B. Bright Boutique** (pilates, barre, cycling, dance)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f6f1ee`, ink `#1a1716`, brand `#1a1716`, accent `#ff6f61` (accent-ink `#1a0b09`), deep `#1a1716`. Fonts **Sora** + **DM Sans**.
**C. Personal Coach** (trainers, nutrition, online coaching)
- paper `#fbfbf9`, brand `#123a2f`, accent `#f2b632` (accent-ink `#1a1305`), deep `#0b241d`. Fonts **Plus Jakarta Sans** + **Inter**. Founder-forward, transformation stories (real only).

## Homepage order
Header (Free trial button) → Hero (action photo/video poster, imperative H1: "Get stronger in {City}", subline: classes/coaching type + beginner-friendly, CTAs, proof: members/years/rating) → Programs/classes cards → How it works (Book trial → Meet coach → Start) → Coaches → Results/testimonials → Schedule preview or "classes 7 days a week" band → Pricing/memberships (if public) → FAQ (beginners? what to bring? contracts?) → CTA band → Location → Footer.

## Avoid
Intimidating-only imagery (show beginners too), unreadable condensed body text, fake transformation photos, hiding price structure entirely.
