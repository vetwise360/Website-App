# Legal, Accounting & Financial

**Audience**: people facing a high-stakes decision (injury, divorce, estate, taxes, retirement) or business owners. They judge *authority, specialisation, results, and approachability*.
**Primary conversion**: Free consultation / Schedule a call · secondary: Call.

## Pick one direction
**A. Modern Authority** (default — law firms, CPAs)
- paper `#fbfaf7`, surface `#f1eee8`, ink `#14181f`, muted `#545a63`, line `#e3ded5`, brand `#14233c` (ink navy), brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#b8893b` (muted gold, accent-ink `#1a1306`), deep `#0e1829`.
- Fonts: **Cormorant Garamond** or **Newsreader** display (500–600, large sizes) + **Inter** body. Radius `0.375rem` (crisp), thin rules, editorial layout with large serif numerals.
**B. Approachable Advisor** (financial planners, insurance, family/estate law)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f5f3`, ink `#16231f`, brand `#1f4d3f`, accent `#e0a43c` (accent-ink `#1a1206`), deep `#12302a`. Fonts **Source Serif 4** + **Albert Sans**. Warm portrait photography.
**C. Aggressive Results** (personal injury, criminal defense)
- paper `#ffffff`, ink `#0b0f17`, brand `#0b1f3a`, accent `#d4372c` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#070f1f`. Fonts **Archivo** (800) + **Inter**. Big result numbers (only real ones), "No fee unless we win" if true.

## Homepage order
Header → Hero (attorney/team portrait or architectural image, H1 specialisation + city: "Estate planning attorneys for {City} families", proof: years / cases / bar memberships, CTAs) → Practice areas (cards, 3–6) → Results / case outcomes (only real) or credentials band → About the firm / attorney bios (photo, education, bar admissions) → Process ("Free consult → Strategy → Representation") → Testimonials → FAQ (cost, timelines, what to bring) → CTA band → Contact (form: name, phone, email, brief description; confidentiality note) → Footer with disclaimer line ("Attorney advertising. Prior results do not guarantee…").

## Trust signals
Bar admissions, Super Lawyers/AV Rated (only if real), CPA/CFP/CFA designations, fiduciary statement, years, real results, professional association logos, confidentiality reassurance.

## Avoid
Gavels, scales of justice clip-art, handshake stock, columns, over-gold gaudy palettes, walls of legalese, invented results.
