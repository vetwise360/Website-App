# Home Services & Trades

**Audience**: homeowners with a problem (often urgent) or a project. They scan for *phone number, service area, trust (licensed/insured, reviews), and speed*. Most traffic is mobile.
**Primary conversion**: Call now / Get a free quote · secondary the other one. Emergency services → phone first.

## Pick one direction
**A. Bold Dependable** (default — plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f3f5f8`, ink `#0d1726`, muted `#4a5668`, line `#e3e7ee`, brand `#0c2d57` (navy), brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#ff6b1a` (safety orange, accent-ink `#1a0d05`), deep `#081c36`.
- Fonts: **Archivo** display (700–800, slightly condensed feel) + **Inter** body. Radius `0.75rem`, buttons `0.625rem` (not pill — sturdier).
**B. Natural Craft** (landscaping, outdoor, pool, painting, remodeling)
- paper `#fbfaf6`, surface `#eef0e8`, ink `#1b2416`, brand `#2f5d3a`, accent `#e2b33f` (accent-ink `#1c1606`), deep `#18301e`. Fonts **Bricolage Grotesque** + **Public Sans**. Big full-bleed project photography.
**C. Clean & Bright** (cleaning, maids, pest, window washing)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#eef7fb`, ink `#0b2433`, brand `#0e7ab8`, accent `#22c55e` (accent-ink `#05210f`), deep `#08324a`. Fonts **Outfit** + **DM Sans**. Airy, lots of white.

## Homepage order
Top utility bar (service area · "Licensed & insured" · phone) → Header (big phone + "Free quote") → Hero (real truck/team/job photo, H1 "{Service} in {City} — done right, on time", bullets: same-day / upfront pricing / warranty; CTAs; proof: rating + years) → Trust bar (licensed, insured, BBB, manufacturer certs, warranty) → Services grid (with photo or icon, link) → Why us (3–4 concrete promises: arrive on time, upfront pricing, clean-up, guarantee) → Recent work / gallery (before-after if real) → Reviews → Service areas (town chips) → Process (Call → Estimate → Done) → FAQ (pricing, emergencies, financing, warranty) → CTA band with phone huge → Contact form (name, phone, service, zip) → Footer.

## Trust signals
License #, insured, years, family-owned, warranty/guarantee, financing, named technicians, service area list, real job photos, Google rating (if real).

## Copy tone
Direct, confident, plain. Numbers and promises. "No surprise fees." CTA: "Get my free quote", "Call (555) …".

## Avoid
Tiny phone numbers, stock "smiling plumber with thumbs up", clip-art tools, walls of text, hiding service area, generic blue-and-white with no accent.
