# Retail & Small E-commerce

**Audience**: shoppers browsing for taste and specific items; locals checking hours and what's in stock. They want *the products, the brand feel, how to buy (online / visit), and reasons to trust*.
**Primary conversion**: Shop now / Visit the store / Order online · secondary: Call, Get directions.

## Pick one direction
**A. Curated Boutique** (default — fashion, gifts, jewelry, home goods, florists)
- paper `#f8f5f0`, surface `#efe9e0`, ink `#1e1b18`, muted `#615850`, line `#e3dbcf`, brand `#2e2a26`, brand-ink `#f8f5f0`, accent `#b5543a` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#1e1b18`.
- Fonts: **Fraunces** (or **Cormorant Garamond** for jewelers) + **Albert Sans**. Product photography on soft neutral backgrounds, editorial lookbook layout.
**B. Bold Brand Shop** (DTC, specialty foods, outdoor)
- paper `#ffffff`, brand client hue, accent a contrasting complementary hue, deep near-black. Fonts **Bricolage Grotesque** + **DM Sans**. Big product hero, color-block categories.

## Homepage order
Header (Shop / Visit / cart icon if e-com) → Hero (hero product or storefront, short brand promise, CTAs) → Featured categories (3–4 image tiles) → Best sellers / new arrivals (product cards: image, name, price — real) → Story/maker section → Reviews → Visit us (photo of shop, hours, address) or shipping/returns promises → Newsletter/Instagram band → Footer.

## Avoid
Cluttered banners, sale-screaming red everywhere, stock lifestyle images, hiding store hours, low-quality product shots without treatment.
