# Automotive

**Audience**: drivers with a problem (warning light, accident, service due) or a purchase. They want *trust (honest pricing, certified techs), speed, location, and to book/call*.
**Primary conversion**: Schedule service / Get a quote · secondary: Call. Towing → phone only.

## Pick one direction
**A. Precision Garage** (default — repair, tires, body shops)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f3f5`, ink `#0f1115`, muted `#4d5360`, line `#e1e3e8`, brand `#111827`, brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#e11d2e` (racing red, accent-ink `#fff`) or `#facc15` (accent-ink `#111`), deep `#0a0c10`.
- Fonts: **Archivo** (700–800, tight) or **Red Hat Display** + **Inter**. Industrial details: thin grid lines, mono-style labels, checkered/diagonal subtle patterns.
**B. Showroom Gloss** (detailing, ceramic coating, dealers, performance)
- paper `#0b0c0e` dark, surface `#141619`, ink `#f2f3f5`, muted `#a2a7b0`, brand `#f2f3f5`, brand-ink `#0b0c0e`, accent `#3b82f6` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#000`. Fonts **Sora** + **Inter**. Reflective hero photography, before/after sliders via CSS.

## Homepage order
Header (phone + "Schedule service") → Hero (shop/bay/car photo, H1 "Honest auto repair in {City}", bullets: ASE-certified / warranty / same-day, CTAs, proof: rating + years) → Trust bar (ASE, AAA approved, warranty months/miles, brands serviced) → Services grid (brakes, oil, diagnostics, AC, tires, inspections) → Why us (transparent quotes, digital inspections, loaner/shuttle) → Reviews → Process → FAQ → CTA band → Hours/location → Footer.

## Avoid
Clip-art wrenches, cheesy flames/chrome, tiny hours, stock thumbs-up mechanic, fake "100% satisfaction" stats.
