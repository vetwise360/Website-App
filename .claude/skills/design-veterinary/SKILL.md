# Veterinary & Pet Care

**Audience**: pet parents (mostly women 28–55) choosing a vet for a new pet, after a move, or in a worry moment. Emotion: anxious → reassured. They decide on *warmth + competence + convenience*.
**Primary conversion**: Book an appointment (online booking link or form) · secondary: Call. Emergency/urgent line if offered.

## Pick one direction
**A. Warm Modern Clinic** (default)
- Tokens: paper `#fbf8f4`, surface `#f3ece3`, ink `#1f2a2e`, muted `#55626a`, line `#e7ddd1`, brand `#1f5f5b` (deep teal), brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#f08a4b` (warm apricot), accent-ink `#1f1a17`, deep `#163f3c`.
- Fonts: display **Fraunces** (soft, optical-size, weight 500–600), body **Figtree**.
- Radius: cards `1.5rem`, buttons pill. Organic shapes: rounded image masks, a subtle paw/leaf-free blob behind hero photo.
**B. Clean Medical-Grade** (specialty/emergency hospitals, referral centers)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f6f8`, ink `#0e2330`, brand `#0b5c8a`, accent `#14b8a6` (accent-ink `#05221f`), deep `#0b2638`. Fonts: **Plus Jakarta Sans** display + **Inter** body. Radius `0.875rem`.
**C. Playful Boutique** (groomers, daycare, pet stores)
- paper `#fffaf2`, brand `#2d3a8c`, accent `#ffb703`, deep `#1d2460`. Fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque** + **DM Sans**. Chunky radius `2rem`, sticker-style badges.

## Homepage order
Header (phone + "Book online") → Hero (real pet/vet photo, H1 about care + town, proof line: years / "Fear Free Certified" / AAHA if true) → Trust bar (AAHA, Fear Free, years, Google rating if real) → Services grid (Wellness & vaccines, Dental, Surgery, Diagnostics, Urgent care, Grooming/boarding if offered) → "Meet the team" (vet photos + names + one line) → New clients section (what to expect at the first visit, 3 steps) → Testimonials → FAQ (new patients? exotic pets? payment plans/insurance? emergencies?) → CTA band → Hours & location (map link, parking) → Footer.

## Trust signals
Named veterinarians with credentials (DVM), Fear Free / AAHA accreditation, years serving the town, species treated, payment options (CareCredit, pet insurance), emergency protocol, clean facility photos.

## Copy tone
Warm, calm, competent. Talk about the pet by name-type ("your dog", "your cat"). Headline formulas: "Compassionate care for every stage of your pet's life" → make specific: "Gentle, thorough vet care in {Town} — since {year}." CTA: "Book a visit".

## Imagery
Real clinic photos > everything. Crop tight on animals' faces, warm grading. No cartoon paws, no clip-art bones, no stock "vet holding puppy" if the clinic has its own photos.

## Avoid
Clinical cold blue-on-white (unless direction B), cartoonish fonts for medical clinics, burying hours/phone, emergency info hidden in footer.
