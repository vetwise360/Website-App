# Restaurants & Hospitality

**Audience**: locals and visitors deciding *where to go tonight* — on a phone. They want to *see the food/space, the menu, hours, location, and book/order* in seconds.
**Primary conversion**: Reserve a table / Order online / Book a stay · secondary: View menu, Call.

## Pick one direction
**A. Editorial Appetite** (default — restaurants, bistros, wine bars)
- paper `#f7f2ea`, surface `#efe6d8`, ink `#1d1915`, muted `#5c5248`, line `#e2d6c4`, brand `#5b1f1a` (oxblood), brand-ink `#f7f2ea`, accent `#d9a441` (accent-ink `#1d1406`), deep `#1a1412`.
- Fonts: **Fraunces** or **Playfair Display** (italic moments) display + **Work Sans** body. Large full-bleed food photography, magazine-style captions, small caps labels.
**B. Casual & Vibrant** (cafes, taquerias, pizza, breweries, food trucks)
- paper `#fffaf0`, brand `#1f3b2c`, accent `#ff5a36` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#13241b`. Fonts **Bricolage Grotesque** (heavy) + **DM Sans**. Bold color blocks, rotated stickers, playful but legible.
**C. Quiet Luxury Stay** (hotels, inns, venues, fine dining)
- paper `#f5f3ef`, ink `#1b1b1a`, brand `#2c3a33`, accent `#a8865a` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#151a17`. Fonts **Cormorant Garamond** + **Albert Sans**. Huge imagery, minimal text, slow reveals.

## Homepage order
Header (Reserve/Order button + hours today) → Full-bleed hero (best food/space photo, short evocative H1 + location, CTAs Reserve + View menu) → Intro / story (chef or owner, 2–3 sentences) → Menu highlights (4–6 dishes with photo, name, short description; link to full menu) → Experience/gallery (asymmetric grid) → Reviews/press quotes → Private events/catering (if offered) → Hours & location (big, with map link and parking) → Final CTA → Footer (socials prominent).

## Trust signals
Press mentions, real review quotes, chef credentials, years open, local sourcing, awards (only real).

## Copy tone
Sensory and concise. "Wood-fired. Seasonal. Right on Main Street." No paragraphs over 3 lines.

## Avoid
PDF-only menus, auto-playing music/video with sound, stock food, dark text on dark photos, tiny hours, carousels as the only way to see dishes.
