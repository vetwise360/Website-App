# Medical, Dental & Healthcare

**Audience**: patients seeking a provider (new to area, insurance change, urgent pain). They need *credibility, accepted insurance, location, and easy booking*.
**Primary conversion**: Request/Book appointment · secondary: Call. For therapy/mental health: "Schedule a free consultation".

## Pick one direction
**A. Calm Clinical Premium** (default — dental, family practice, optometry)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f4f7f6`, ink `#122a2a`, muted `#4f6362`, line `#e2e9e8`, brand `#0f5e5a`, brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#e3a33b` (accent-ink `#231a08`), deep `#0b3332`.
- Fonts: **Manrope** display (600–700) + **Inter** body. Radius `1rem`.
**B. Boutique Wellness Practice** (cosmetic dentistry, chiro, PT, functional medicine)
- paper `#faf7f2`, surface `#f0e9df`, ink `#2a2420`, brand `#5a6b4e` (sage), accent `#c56a45` (terracotta, accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#2e3329`. Fonts: **Newsreader** display + **Albert Sans** body.
**C. Mental Health / Therapy**
- Soft and quiet: paper `#fbfaf8`, brand `#3f5a6b`, accent `#d99a6c`, deep `#24333c`. Fonts **Literata** + **Work Sans**. Lots of whitespace, no stock smiling-crowd imagery.

## Homepage order
Header → Hero (provider/office photo, H1 outcome: "Healthy smiles for the whole family in {Town}", subhead with services + "Accepting new patients", CTAs, proof: years / rating / "Most insurance accepted") → Insurance & trust strip (logos of accepted insurers as text chips if no logos, associations ADA/ACA) → Services (grouped: preventive, restorative, cosmetic / or conditions treated) → Meet the doctor(s) (photo, credentials, short human bio) → What to expect / first visit (3 steps) → Testimonials → Technology/office (if notable) → FAQ (insurance, emergencies, new patients, payment plans) → CTA band → Location/hours → Footer.

## Trust signals
Credentials (DDS, MD, DPT, LCSW), board certifications, years, insurance list, before/after (cosmetic, only if real), privacy/HIPAA-friendly language for forms, accessibility of office.

## Copy tone
Reassuring, plain-language, never fear-based. Avoid medical claims you can't verify.

## Avoid
Stock teeth/syringe images, sterile hospital blue overload, tiny phone number, forms asking for medical details (keep to name/phone/reason).
