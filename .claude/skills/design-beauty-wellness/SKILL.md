# Beauty, Spa & Wellness

**Audience**: clients buying a feeling and a result; they browse on Instagram and phones. They want *the vibe, the work (portfolio), prices/services, and instant booking*.
**Primary conversion**: Book now (booking platform link) · secondary: View services & prices.

## Pick one direction
**A. Soft Luxe** (default — spas, med spas, salons)
- paper `#faf6f1`, surface `#f1e8de`, ink `#2a2320`, muted `#6a5d55`, line `#e6d9cc`, brand `#6e4b3a` (mocha), brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#c99a7a` → use a deeper `#9c5f43` for buttons (accent-ink `#fff`) to keep contrast, deep `#2b201b`.
- Fonts: **Cormorant Garamond** (or **Instrument Serif** via `@fontsource/instrument-serif`) display + **Albert Sans** body. Large italic serif words, thin lines, arched image masks (`rounded-t-full`).
**B. Editorial Edge** (barbers, tattoo, modern hair studios)
- paper `#f4f3f0`, ink `#111111`, brand `#111111`, accent `#c8a96a` (accent-ink `#111`), deep `#0c0c0c`. Fonts **Space Grotesk** or **Archivo** (uppercase, tracking) + **Inter**. High-contrast black/white photography, grid portfolio.
**C. Fresh Wellness** (yoga, pilates, massage, holistic)
- paper `#f7f8f3`, brand `#56664c` (sage), accent `#d08c60` (accent-ink `#1f130b`), deep `#2a3226`. Fonts **Newsreader** + **Figtree**. Calm, airy, natural light photos.

## Homepage order
Header (Book now) → Hero (signature photo, short sensory H1, subline with location, Book CTA, proof: rating/years/"by appointment") → Services & pricing highlights (categories with "from $X" if real) → Portfolio/gallery (masonry, real work) → About the team/artists → Reviews → First-visit/policies (booking, cancellation, parking) → Gift cards/memberships (if offered) → Instagram-style strip linking to socials → Location & hours → Footer.

## Avoid
Generic stock models, too many script fonts, low-contrast beige-on-beige text (check `muted` contrast!), hidden prices if they're public elsewhere.
