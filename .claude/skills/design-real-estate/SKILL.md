# Real Estate, Builders & Interiors

**Audience**: buyers, sellers, owners/investors. They judge *taste, local expertise, track record, and responsiveness*.
**Primary conversion**: Get a free home valuation / Book a consultation / View listings · secondary: Call/text agent.

## Pick one direction
**A. Architectural Minimal** (default — luxury agents, builders, architects, interiors)
- paper `#f6f5f2`, surface `#ecebe6`, ink `#151515`, muted `#5b5b58`, line `#dcdad3`, brand `#151515`, brand-ink `#f6f5f2`, accent `#9a7b4f` (bronze, accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#111111`.
- Fonts: **Instrument Sans** or **Geist** (light/regular at huge sizes) + same family body, or **EB Garamond** display for heritage brands. Full-bleed photography, thin rules, numbered sections, lots of negative space.
**B. Friendly Local Expert** (residential agents, teams)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f5f7`, ink `#13202b`, brand `#1c3d5a`, accent `#e07a3f` (accent-ink `#1a0d05`), deep `#10263a`. Fonts **Manrope** + **Inter**. Agent portrait prominent.
**C. Property Management / Investor**
- paper `#ffffff`, brand `#173f35`, accent `#d4a63a` (accent-ink `#1a1305`), deep `#0f2a23`. Fonts **Plus Jakarta Sans** + **Inter**. Clear owner vs tenant paths.

## Homepage order
Header → Hero (best property/interior photo, H1: "{City} homes, sold with care" / project-led for builders, CTAs, proof: homes sold / volume / years — real only) → Featured listings or projects (3, large cards) → Services (buy / sell / valuation / or design-build steps) → About the agent/team (portrait, local roots) → Neighborhoods/areas served → Testimonials → Valuation/lead form band ("What's my home worth?") → Contact → Footer (license #, brokerage, Equal Housing logo text).

## Avoid
Cluttered IDX-style grids on the homepage, stock skyline, cheesy key-handover photos, too many accent colors.
