# Professional, B2B & Industrial

**Audience**: decision-makers comparing vendors. They look for *specialisation, capabilities, credibility (clients, certifications, case studies), and a clear way to start a conversation*.
**Primary conversion**: Request a quote / Book a consultation · secondary: View capabilities / case studies.

## Pick one direction
**A. Confident Corporate Modern** (default — consulting, staffing, B2B services)
- paper `#ffffff`, surface `#f4f5f2`, ink `#111613`, muted `#525a55`, line `#e2e5df`, brand `#173a2f` (or client hue deepened), brand-ink `#fff`, accent `#e9a23b` (accent-ink `#1a1306`), deep `#0d221b`.
- Fonts: **Manrope** or **Albert Sans** display + **Inter** body. Editorial grids, big numbers, case-study cards with outcome metrics.
**B. Industrial Strength** (manufacturing, engineering, logistics, commercial construction)
- paper `#f5f5f3`, surface `#e9e9e5`, ink `#121212`, brand `#1b2a38`, accent `#f2a900` (safety amber, accent-ink `#121212`), deep `#0e161e`. Fonts **Archivo** (condensed feel, uppercase eyebrows) + **Public Sans**. Full-bleed facility/project photography, spec-sheet style data rows, certification badges (ISO, OSHA).
**C. Creative Studio** (marketing/design agencies, architects, photographers)
- paper `#f3f1ec`, ink `#141414`, brand `#141414`, accent `#ff5b2e` (accent-ink `#fff`), deep `#141414`. Fonts **Bricolage Grotesque** or **Instrument Sans** + **Inter**. Work-first, oversized type, case-study tiles.

## Homepage order
Header → Hero (clear specialisation + industry served: "Precision CNC machining for aerospace & medical", CTAs, proof: years / clients / certifications) → Client/industry logos → Services/capabilities (cards or spec rows) → Industries served → Case studies / featured projects (outcome metrics, real) → Why us / process (4 steps) → Certifications & equipment (industrial) or team (consulting) → Testimonials → FAQ (lead times, minimums, engagement models) → CTA band → Contact (RFQ form: name, company, email, details) → Footer.

## Avoid
Handshake/stock-meeting photos, buzzword soup ("synergy", "solutions"), unclear what they actually do, tiny contact info.
